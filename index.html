About Us

The Sacred Town of Rajasthan

Pushkar is one of India's oldest and most sacred pilgrimage towns, located in the rugged Aravalli Hills of Rajasthan, about 14 km from Ajmer. Famous for its tranquil holy lake and rare Brahma Temple, the city flawlessly blends intense spiritual devotion with vibrant desert culture and adventure.

Whether you seek spiritual enlightenment, cultural immersion, or desert adventure, Pushkar offers an unforgettable experience that touches the soul and ignites the senses.

Pushkar Lake

The spiritual heart of the town, surrounded by 52 bathing ghats. Walk barefoot along the lake's edge and experience the mesmerizing evening Aarti ceremony at Varaha Ghat or Brahma Ghat.

Jagatpita Brahma Temple

One of the very few existing temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma, the Hindu creator god. A cornerstone of Vedic history with its distinct red spire and four-faced idol.

history 14th Century verified Rare Shrine

Savitri Mata Temple

Situated atop Ratnagiri Hill, it offers panoramic views of the town and lake. Reach it via the Savitri Mata Ropeway or a scenic hike of 750 steps.

Camel Safari & Camping

The desert outskirts feature great opportunities for desert camping and sunset camel safaris. Experience the golden dunes and starlit nights of the Thar Desert.
